About UmamTek

Built in Bhagalpur,
for Bhagalpur.

UmamTek exists to solve a simple, everyday problem: finding a skilled worker you can trust, without the wait and without the guesswork.

Our Story

Why we started UmamTek

Finding a reliable electrician, plumber, or carpenter in Bhagalpur has traditionally meant asking around, waiting on word-of-mouth, and hoping the person who shows up is who you expected. UmamTek was built to change that.

We connect homes and institutions across Bhagalpur with verified, skilled workers — electricians, plumbers, painters, carpenters, drivers, teachers, and more — through a single, simple booking channel.

Every worker on the platform is checked before they're listed, every job is tracked from confirmation to completion, and support is available around the clock — because a broken tap or a wiring fault doesn't wait for business hours.

What We Stand For

The principles behind every booking

Trust & Verification

Workers are ID-checked before they're allowed to take a job through UmamTek.

Speed & Reliability

Fast matching and live tracking, so you're never left wondering when help arrives.

Fair Treatment for Workers

We aim to build a platform where skilled workers get consistent, fairly-arranged work.

Local Roots

Registered and rooted in Bhagalpur — we know the city we're building for.

Who We Serve
