About UMAMTEK

Built on-site, trade by trade — not in a boardroom.

UMAMTEK connects Bhagalpur's homes, offices and institutions with verified electricians, technicians and contractors, backed by transparent pricing and a documented service record.

Our story

Why UMAMTEK exists

Most people hire electricians, plumbers and technicians on word of mouth — with no record of the work, no transparent pricing, and no way to hold anyone accountable if something goes wrong. UMAMTEK was started to fix that: every worker is matched by skill category, every job comes with a documented rate card, and every booking is tracked from request to invoice.
