The Process

From request to
completed work.

Five steps, one WhatsApp thread. Here's exactly what happens when you book through UmamTek.

01

Tell us what you need

Message us on WhatsApp or use the booking form — share the service (electrician, plumber, driver, etc.), your area in Bhagalpur, and your preferred time.

02

We match you with a worker

We assign a verified, ID-checked worker from our Bhagalpur network based on the job and your location.

03

Get confirmation & live tracking

You receive the worker's name and estimated arrival time, with a live tracking link so you know exactly when they'll reach you.

04

Work gets done, then rated

Review the completed job, pay through your preferred method, and rate your experience — it helps keep quality high across the platform.
