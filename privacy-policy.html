Privacy Policy & Terms

LAST UPDATED: 25 AUGUST 2026

1. Information we collect

When you create an account, book a service, file a complaint, or contact us, we collect your name, mobile number, service address, and — where you choose to share it — your live location, so we can dispatch the right technician to you.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your UMAMTEK account.
  • To match, dispatch and track technicians for your booking.
  • To generate invoices and maintain your service history.
  • To contact you about bookings, complaints, or support requests.

3. OTP & account security

Your mobile number is verified using a one-time password (OTP) sent via SMS. We do not store the OTP itself. Your account password is stored securely by our authentication provider and is never visible to UMAMTEK staff in plain text.

4. Sharing of information

We share your booking details (name, phone, address) only with the technician assigned to your job, so the work can be carried out. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Data retention

We retain your account and booking history for as long as your account is active, so you can view past invoices and service records. You may request deletion of your account by contacting us.

6. Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by writing to owner@umamtek.com.

7. Payments

UMAMTEK currently supports "pay after work" for most services. Where online payment is enabled, transactions are processed by our payment partner and UMAMTEK does not store your card or UPI credentials.

8. Terms of service

By booking a service through UMAMTEK, you agree to the estimated pricing shown at the time of booking, which may vary based on the actual scope of work required on site. Cancellations should be made as early as possible to avoid inconvenience to the assigned technician.

9. Jurisdiction

All disputes arising from the use of UMAMTEK's services are subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts in Bhagalpur, Bihar.

10. Contact us

For any questions about this policy, reach us at owner@umamtek.com or call +91 90657 60751.
